Your current challenges

What CAS Services told us about how things run today, alongside how BigChange addresses each one.

The challenge
The BigChange solution

Booking work ahead without an assigned operative

Connecteam makes it hard to book a job without assigning a person, so jobs go into Google Calendar first and are re-entered into Connecteam day by day, rather than planned a week or more ahead.

The BigChange solution

Jobs can be created with no date or person, a date but no person, or fully booked. Anything not yet allocated sits in one Unscheduled Jobs list, so work can be planned weekly or monthly from a single place with no Google Calendar in between.

Recurring window cleans held manually

Quarterly and other regular cleans are typed into Google Calendar to keep track, then pulled across into Connecteam to issue to the team.

The BigChange solution

Recurring work is set up once with its recurrence pattern and lives in BigChange permanently. Schedule All can then plan the upcoming visits in bulk, up to a thousand jobs at a time, respecting skills, availability and travel.

Several systems, and a business that has outgrown them

Systems that worked for a smaller CAS are now stretched, with "a big company at a small company scale" and more acquisitions to come. Clearground uses one system where CAS uses several to do the same thing.

The BigChange solution

One platform covering scheduling, the field app, fleet, equipment, compliance, automation and invoicing, already running for businesses from Clearground up to Sunbelt Rentals, EDF Energy and Lanes Group, so it scales as the group grows.

Following a job from request to invoice

The goal is to see every job through: who is assigned and when, whether it was successful, whether more work is needed, completion, sign-off and invoice.

The BigChange solution

Each job has its own folder and a status that updates live on the schedule as the operative progresses. Results, follow-on work, sign-off and the invoice are all captured against the job, with invoices syncing to Xero.

Equipment going missing

Dehumidifiers, scaffold towers, deadweight trolleys and collapsible ladders move between vans, sites and the office, and their whereabouts rely on word of mouth. More kit, such as a cherry picker, is on the way.

The BigChange solution

A clean, clear record of every piece of equipment, who has it and where it went. QR codes or barcodes are scanned on pick-up and drop-off, the last person to scan it is always logged, and service dates are tracked so expensive kit stays maintained.

No proof RAMS have been read

RAMS can be attached in Connecteam, but there is no way of knowing whether operatives actually open them.

The BigChange solution

RAMS can be made a mandatory step the operative has to open and sign before the job can continue, with the time recorded against the job for a full audit trail.

Waiting hours for answers from the field

Pass or fail results on building testing systems, or an arrival estimate, can take up to three hours to come back.

The BigChange solution

Messages can be sent from the office with preset reply options, so the operative answers with one tap. Live ETAs are also available on the schedule without needing to ask.

Field teams relying on the office for everything

Operatives currently depend on the office for their next job, site information and answers, for example when window cleaners are stood down by the wind and need something else to pick up. Every call slows the job down and interrupts the office.

The BigChange solution

Operatives self-serve from the app: they see outstanding work filtered to their skills and sorted by distance, allocate jobs to themselves, and have site history, documents and equipment locations to hand. Work orders are completed quickly and efficiently without waiting on the office.

Qualifications tracked on a spreadsheet

A spreadsheet has to be checked daily to make sure nothing has expired, and it is not always clear who is qualified to do what.

The BigChange solution

Skills, certifications and cards (such as rope access cards) are held on each operative's profile with expiry dates and automatic reminders. The schedule warns you if a job is given to someone without the required skill.

Routes planned by hand

The maintenance manager works out who goes where across London from memory and a map, and ad-hoc requests from the office can pull operatives across the city.

The BigChange solution

Schedule Assistant suggests the best person and slot for each job based on diaries, home address, skills and travel, and Route Optimiser reorders a day's jobs into the most efficient route in one click.

Revisits falling through the cracks

When an operative finds more work is needed, the return visit can be overtaken by other jobs and forgotten until a property manager or resident chases.

The BigChange solution

Automations turn a job result such as "parts required" into a new follow-on job automatically, flagged and filed in its own list so nothing is lost. Alerts can then keep customers and the office informed.

Visibility over company vans

Vans are signwritten and fuel is paid by CAS, with personal use allowed. There is no record of how vans are driven, mileage outside work, who had a vehicle when a parking ticket arrived, or condition at handover. Trackers are currently sitting unused.

The BigChange solution

Hardwired tracking installed by BigChange, with a daily driver behaviour score, a mileage report split between working and non-working hours, and full journey history for any vehicle on any date. Enforced vehicle checks with photos record condition every week.

The office fielding app support calls

Operatives with app issues call the office, interrupting more important work when the office can only pass the problem on.

The BigChange solution

Operatives contact BigChange support directly from the app and speak to the Leeds-based team, who resolve it there and then.

Manual data entry when moving systems

Previous moves meant importing all jobs and records by hand.

The BigChange solution

BigChange assists with data transfer: data is exported in CSV format, then cleansed, formatted and moved together with a dedicated onboarding specialist, with 24 hours of one-to-one time, remotely or in person at our Leeds office.

What we showed you today

Each area of the platform we walked through on the call. Select a topic to see the detail.

The BigChange App

Two sides to the system: the office platform and a simple app your operatives use on any Android or iOS phone or tablet.

  • Simple by design: big, clear buttons that work the same in portrait or landscape.
  • Controlled visibility: you decide how far ahead each operative can see their work, from a month ahead to only the next job.
  • A folder for every job: customer and site details, access information, notes and attachments such as RAMS, all in one place.
  • Live in both directions: a photo or video taken on site appears instantly in the office, and any change made in the office (such as a new time) appears instantly on the app.
  • Hardware available: tablets with van cradles and SIM cards can be supplied if required.

Enforced workflows and RAMS

The system can make sure the right steps happen in the right order on every job.

  • RAMS that must be opened: operatives are forced to open and sign RAMS, with the time recorded against the job.
  • A linear process: for example: no job without a vehicle check, no photos without a risk assessment, no closing a job without photos.
  • Next job held back: operatives can be prevented from seeing or starting the next job until the current one is closed down.
  • Information always captured: whatever you need from each visit is collected every time, protecting the business.

Unscheduled work on the app

Operatives can see work waiting to be allocated and pick it up themselves.

  • Sorted by distance: shows outstanding jobs based on where the operative is right now.
  • Filtered by skill: operatives only see work they are qualified for, so window cleaners and maintenance operatives see their own jobs.
  • Self-allocation: open a job, check the details and assign it, with no call to the office needed.
  • You decide: which jobs appear here and which operatives have access.
Useful for window cleaners stood down by the wind and maintenance operatives who finish early.

Messaging with quick replies

Work communication centralised in the platform, alongside WhatsApp rather than replacing it.

  • Preset reply options: add options such as arrival times or pass and fail, and the operative replies with a single tap.
  • Shared visibility: any member of the office team can pick up a reply if the sender is out.
  • Free text too: operatives can still reply in their own words when needed.

Vehicle tracking and driver behaviour

Tracking integrated with the platform, covering vans and operatives who travel by other means.

  • Installed for you: BigChange fits proper hardwired trackers in the vans.
  • Device tracking: operatives who do not drive company vans are tracked through their device.
  • Live ETAs: share arrival times with customers when an operative is on the way.
  • Driver behaviour score: updated daily from a range of driving metrics, for passive management with no awkward conversations.
  • Working and non-working mileage: an instant report showing miles driven inside and outside set hours.

Absences and vehicle checks

Day-to-day admin handled from the app.

  • Absences from the app: holiday and absence requests block out the schedule automatically, however far ahead.
  • Enforced vehicle checks: for example every Monday morning: walk round, take photos, confirm no damage and sign off.
  • Clear responsibility: a dated record of condition at every check, supporting your legal duty to keep company vehicles safe.

Equipment tracking

A clear record of where every piece of kit is and who has it.

  • Find the nearest: search for a dehumidifier, for example, and see which operative has one and how far away, plus how many are in the warehouse.
  • QR codes and barcodes: scanned on pick-up and drop-off to log each movement, with the last person to scan it always on record.
  • Visible to all: the same view for operatives in the field and the office.

Customer history on the app

Basic CRM access for operatives, without any financial information.

  • Site profiles: customer details and access information for any site.
  • Previous jobs: past job reports and photos, so a different operative arriving for the first time has the full picture.
  • Less back and forth: ideal where one person quotes and another carries out the work.

Expenses, documents and support

Everything else operatives need, in the same app.

  • Expenses: logged from the app with photos of receipts.
  • Document library: a virtual notice board in folders, for insurance, certifications, policies and more.
  • Leeds-based support: operatives call BigChange directly from the app, not the office.

Fleet and resources

Where your operational information lives.

  • Vehicle folders: MOT, road tax and service dates with automatic reminders, plus V5s, service history and agreements stored against each vehicle.
  • Resource groups: split operatives into maintenance, window cleaning, rope access or subcontractors, and filter the schedule by group.
  • Skills and certifications: held with expiry dates and reminders; the schedule warns if a job is given to someone without the right skill.
  • Attachments and rates: store cards, insurance and documents, plus hours and hourly rates.
  • Productivity at a glance: hours worked and miles driven per operative for any given day.

The map

A visual view of your whole operation.

  • Find any vehicle or operative: by registration or by name.
  • Journey history: every leg logged with times, stops, mileage, cost and speeding incidents, with street view of where events happened.
  • Jobs on the map: see upcoming and unscheduled jobs plotted together, filtered by type such as window cleaning, to group nearby work.
  • Territories and subcontractors: mark out where subcontractors are based, such as your Bristol operative, with a radius around them.
  • Equipment on the map: see where every item of a given type is located.

Automations and alerts

Repetitive processes and communication handled automatically.

  • Follow-on jobs: a "parts required" result can create a new job automatically, flagged and filed in a list of jobs awaiting parts.
  • Customer communication: confirmation emails when a job is scheduled, and a text the night before for residential customers.
  • Internal alerts: for example, notify the office when a vehicle enters the London congestion zone.
  • Quote follow-ups: automatically chase quotes that have been sent but not accepted after a set number of days.
  • Self-serve: we set up the core automations at the start, then you add more as needs arise.

The schedule

A live view of the whole team, built for the big screen in the office.

  • Daily, weekly and monthly views: plan ahead in weekly, monthly or list view and use the daily view as a live feed.
  • Status colours: green completed, red problem, purple in progress, grey yet to start, all updating through the day.
  • Travel time built in: driving time accounts for traffic and flags when an operative will arrive late to the next job.
  • Quick job creation: book a job now, or save it with no date or person to the unscheduled list.

Schedule Assistant and Route Optimiser

Data-driven booking and routing.

  • Schedule Assistant: scans every relevant diary and suggests who can do a job by a given date, and how far they will already be from it.
  • Route Optimiser: reorders a day's jobs into the most efficient order in one click.
  • Real-world constraints: accounts for start and finish locations (including a hotel stay) and fixed appointment times.
  • Room for more work: time saved on the road can create space for an extra job.

List view and Schedule All

Planning in bulk.

  • List view: filter jobs by client, month, flag or any other criteria, such as all Newcastle City Council jobs in October.
  • Schedule All: plan up to a thousand jobs at a time into the most efficient order, respecting skills, diaries and travel.
  • Recurring work: ideal for planning the month's recurring window cleaning and PPM visits in one go.
